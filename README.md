# Weddingly — A UX Case Study

**UX/UI Design Case Study | Araba Adenu-Mensah, UX/UI & Graphic Designer, Lethbridge, AB**

Weddingly is a mobile app prototype that centralises wedding planning and invitations in one place: guest list management, seating, gifting, venue details, colour schemes, locations, and invitations.

The idea came from my sister getting married, and seeing how many separate concerns couples have to juggle at once.

## Overview
The current iteration of Weddingly is a mobile app designed to help couples invite guests to their dream wedding, with features for guest lists, seating, gifting, venue, and colour scheme.

## My process
- **UX research:** Research on wedding industry trends, pain points, and user needs, plus user interviews with potential users
- **Design:** User personas, user flows, and wireframes, followed by high-fidelity mockups and a clickable prototype
- **Testing and iteration:** Tested with a group of beta users, gathered feedback on usability and functionality, and iterated to improve the app

## Next steps and learning
I plan to continue gathering user feedback and improving the design, and to build a second aspect of the app for the planner, not just the invitation side. Designing a successful wedding planning app requires a deep understanding of user needs and pain points, and a focus on a user-friendly, intuitive interface.
